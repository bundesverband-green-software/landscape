Hi! Green Algorithms Calculator is listed in the [Green Software Landscape](https://landscape.bundesverband-green-software.de), a directory of relevant Green Software tools categorized into a landscape. It is run by the [Bundesverband Green Software](https://www.bundesverband-green-software.de/), a German NGO with currently over 30 member companies. The landscape itself is open source: [bundesverband-green-software/landscape](https://github.com/bundesverband-green-software/landscape).

We recently started to provide badges, one per sub-category of the landscape. Green Algorithms Calculator would fit **Planning & Calculation**. It is also listed under **AI Model Training** and **AI Inference**, so other badges may fit as well.
